Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we use

Floxovo uses only the storage needed to run the service:

  • Sign-in session: keeps you signed in between visits. Required for accounts.
  • Theme preference: remembers whether you chose light or dark mode.
  • Guest study sets and progress: saved in your browser's local storage when you aren't signed in.

What we don't use

We don't use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

Managing storage

You can clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Clearing guest storage deletes guest study sets on that device. Signing out ends your session.

Questions

Email support@floxovo.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
