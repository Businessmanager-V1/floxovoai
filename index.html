Common questions

Is Floxovo free?

Yes. The core study tools are free to use. We'll tell you clearly before any paid option is added.

Do I need an account?

No. You can study as a guest, and your sets stay on your device. An account lets you sync across devices.

Is my data safe?

Read our Privacy Policy. Uploaded files aren't stored; only the extracted text is kept with your study set, and you can delete it any time.

Can kids use Floxovo?

Yes, with a parent or guardian involved. Read our children's section and contact us with questions.
