About Floxovo

We build tools that help learners understand what they study, remember it longer, and feel confident doing it.

Our mission

Good learning should not depend on how much time or money you have. Floxovo turns the material you already have, like lessons, notes, and textbook pages, into study tools you can use right away.

What we believe

  • Learning comes first. Our tutor gives hints and questions before answers, because thinking it through is how people remember.
  • Your work is yours. You choose what to upload and can delete it at any time.
  • Built for everyone. Simple language, clear design, and features that work on phones, tablets, and computers.
  • Honest about AI. AI can make mistakes. We say so, and we encourage checking important answers against your teacher or textbook.

Who we are

Floxovo is an independent project. We're a small team of students and builders who wanted better study tools without paywalls or distractions. We're still growing, and feedback from learners, parents, and teachers shapes what we build next.
