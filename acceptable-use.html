Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Use Floxovo for learning

Floxovo is for studying and learning. Please use it respectfully and honestly.

You must not

  • Upload content you don't have the right to use, including copyrighted textbooks or course materials you're not permitted to share.
  • Upload or request content that is hateful, harassing, sexual, violent, or that promotes self-harm or dangerous activities.
  • Share personal information about yourself or others in tutor questions, such as addresses, phone numbers, or photos.
  • Try to access other people's accounts or study sets, or bypass security or usage limits.
  • Attempt to hack, overload, scrape, or disrupt Floxovo, or use it to send spam.
  • Use the tutor to cheat on tests where that is not allowed, or to produce content meant to deceive others.
  • Use Floxovo to harm, bully, or target other people, including other students.

Keeping everyone safe

If you see something that breaks these rules, or you're worried about someone's safety, contact support@floxovo.com. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

What happens if you break these rules

We may remove content, limit features, or suspend or close accounts. Where appropriate, we may report serious issues to the relevant authorities.

Report a copyright concern

If you believe content on Floxovo infringes your copyright, email support@floxovo.com with the material's location, a description of the work, your contact details, and a statement that you are the owner or authorized to act for them. We'll review and respond promptly.
